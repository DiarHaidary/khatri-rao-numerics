 \caption{Finite-size distortion of Gaussian tensor sketches for two
 geometries, each represented by one fixed rank-$8$ subspace at each
 tensor order $d=2$ and $d=3$, with mode dimension
 $n=8$ (ambient dimensions $N=64$ and $N=512$, respectively).
 For an orthonormal basis $U$ and the normalized sketch $\Omega$, distortion is
 $\|U^\top\Omega\Omega^\top U-I_8\|_2$.
 The common-factor subspace is
 $\operatorname{span}\{e_a\otimes e_1\otimes\cdots\otimes e_1:1\le a\le8\}$;
 the second subspace is one independent Haar-distributed subspace fixed
 throughout the experiment for each tensor order.
 Lines show medians and shading shows empirical $10$th--$90$th percentiles
 over $64$ independent probe trials; widths within a trial are nested
 prefixes and both geometries share the same Gaussian factor draws.
 The dashed horizontal line is $\varepsilon=0.5$.
 The lower logarithmic strips mark the sampled width range and the
 sufficient closed-certificate widths $m_{\mathrm{cert}}=33\,968$ and
 $489\,430$, computed at $\delta=0.1$, $\Ksg^2=8/3$, and
 $q=\lceil\log(4r/\delta)\rceil=6$ without fitted constants.
 These certificate widths were not simulated.
 The experiment illustrates geometry at fixed rank and the conservatism
 of this sufficient certificate; it does not establish the asymptotic
 $r\log^{d-1}r$ dimension dependence.
 Seed: $20261007$.}
 \label{fig:kr-numerical-distortion}
