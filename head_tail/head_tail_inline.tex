\caption{A common-factor head with a generic orthogonal tail. Here $N=16r$ (up to 16384), $R=2m$, and $m=3r+1$. The first $r$ right singular vectors form the Gaussian common-factor witness; the other $R-r$ vectors are a fixed Gaussian-QR basis in its orthogonal complement. Head singular values are one and tail singular values are $(R-r)^{-1/2}$, so $\tau_r(A)=1$ and zero output has ratio $r+1$. Mode dimensions are $(r,16)$, $(r,4,4)$, and $(r,2,2,4)$; the common-mode product is 16 throughout. One fixed input per rank is shared across all laws, with 128 trials through $r=64$, 96 at $r=128$, 64 at $r=256$, and 32 each at $r=512,1024$. Lines are medians and bands are empirical 10--90\% quantiles. The dashed line in (a) is the asymptotic dense spectral edge; in (b), it is the dense Gaussian expected-error bound $1.5$. These are not finite-draw bounds for the product sketches. Every sketch and its error are evaluated separately, without an exact output-law transfer.}
